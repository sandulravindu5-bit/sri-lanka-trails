Explore with care

Safer journeys make better memories.

A few simple checks can help you enjoy Sri Lanka with more confidence. Always confirm your transport arrangements and follow local guidance.

🚗

Seat belts & safe driving

Wear a seat belt where fitted, ask the driver to follow road rules, and never pressure a driver to speed. Take breaks on longer journeys.

🛵

Scooter & motorbike care

Wear a properly fastened helmet, check brakes and lights, carry the required licence and insurance, and ride only if you are confident and legally permitted.

📍

Share your route

Keep your accommodation informed of major travel plans, save your destination details offline, and agree on pickup points before setting off.

💳

Confirm costs first

Agree the fare, inclusions, waiting time and payment method before travel. Keep a written confirmation of important arrangements.

🌊

Respect local advice

Check weather and sea conditions, follow wildlife and park instructions, dress appropriately at religious sites, and avoid isolated areas after dark.

🆘

Know how to get help

Save local emergency contacts and your accommodation number. If there is immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
